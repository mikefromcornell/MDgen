      Help & privacy

      Shortcuts

      • Ctrl/Cmd+V paste image anywhere on the page
      • Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+C copy Markdown
      • Ctrl/Cmd+K convert another (reset)
      • Esc cancel running job / close dialog

      Which engine?

      • Local OCR — screenshots, documents, terminal output, articles. Free, instant, nothing leaves your device.
      • AI Vision (BYOK) — handwriting, complex tables, charts, diagrams. Uses your own Gemini key (free tier) or OpenAI key; the image is sent to that provider.

      Accuracy tips

      • Crop to the content; MDgen auto-upscales small text and boosts contrast before OCR.
      • Low OCR confidence? MDgen shows it — retry with AI Vision in one click.
      • Use Compact for tokens before pasting into an LLM to cut tokens ~25%.

      Privacy

      • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no server. History lives in your browser's IndexedDB.
      • AI keys never leave this browser — calls go directly to Google/OpenAI.
